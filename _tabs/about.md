I'm Peter Bode, a 4th year Computer Science student at Rutgers University.
This site documents a semester-long Capture-the-Flag track completed as an
Honors Contract for Computer Security (01:198:419) with Prof. Paul
Krzyzanowski, Fall 2026.

Each post pairs an **attacker walkthrough** (how a vulnerability was found and
exploited) with a **defender's analysis** (root cause, vulnerability class and
CWE, the correct fix, and how the issue would be detected or prevented in
production).

## Ethics and scope

All work is performed exclusively on authorized training platforms such as
CryptoHack, PortSwigger Web Security Academy, pwn.college, and HackTheBox, or
on intentionally vulnerable practice targets in an isolated lab VM. No live or
third-party systems are tested. Posts are sanitized to respect each platform's
rules, so no live flags or prohibited solutions are published.
